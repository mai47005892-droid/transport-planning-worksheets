# Transport Planning Worksheets

Editable CSV worksheets for recording transport-simulation route decisions. These are observation templates, not game mods, a vehicle database, benchmark results, or an official profit formula.

## Files

- `cargo-cost-ledger.csv`: compare two complete delivery chains over the same observation period, keeping investment and financing separate from operations.
- `batch-upgrade-review.csv`: record selected vehicles, successor requirements, platform warnings, baseline service and the result after a change.
- `purchase-checklist.csv`: distinguish essential requirements from optional preferences before a purchase.
- `expectation-review.csv`: record a frustrating task, one local change and the retest outcome.
- `connection-review.md`: a generic record for narrowing down a broken connection without declaring an unverified fix.

## Use

Download an individual CSV and open it in a spreadsheet editor. Preserve the original, add your observations in a copy, and record the game build, platform, mods and observation period. Empty cells mean unknown, not zero. The dates already present in some templates are the original editorial review dates; replace them with your actual observation date when recording a run.

Use the same accounting scope and period for both options. Keep a shared cost in one place only. A line's operating result does not establish the cost of the entire company. Record a change as successful only after repeating the affected task in your own save.

These worksheets accompany the independent guides at [Cargo Route Guide](https://cargorouteguide.com/).
